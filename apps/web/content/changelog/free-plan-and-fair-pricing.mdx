## Plans

- The [Pro page](/pro) now shows the free plan beside Pro and Business, so you can see what you already get.
- Purchasing power parity: where it applies, a banner shows the discount for your country and checkout applies it.
- Annual plans are billed yearly with two months free.
- Pro and Business checkout is live.

## Docs actions

Every [docs](/docs) page has an "On this page" panel with actions: edit it on GitHub, copy it as Markdown, export it as PDF or EPUB, open it in a chat, or connect to the MCP. On small screens the panel folds above the page.
