## Showcase

[Showcase](/create/showcase) is MotionVideo's first creative tool. Drop in screenshots, images or screen recordings, pick a template and export a video that loops without a seam. It's the start of [Create](/create), where MotionVideo's own tools live.

- 21 templates in six collections: carousels, orbits, stacks and grids, depth, one-at-a-time sequences and device scenes.
- Put screenshots in browser, phone, tablet or laptop frames, and let tall screenshots scroll inside them.
- Videos play on the cards. Trim them, and choose whether they loop or hold their last frame.
- Change the look: corner radius, shadow, light and glare, depth of field, camera, backgrounds from solid colours to drifting mesh gradients, and finishing touches like grain and bloom.
- Add a title, corner captions and your logo, animated in and out so the loop stays clean.
- Frame it for any feed with aspect ratios and social presets that show where an app's buttons sit.

## Rendered on your device

Your images, videos and projects stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the video is rendered on your device and downloaded straight away. Projects save as you work, and a project file moves one to another browser.
