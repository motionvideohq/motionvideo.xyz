## API reference

The [API reference](/docs/api) now has a page for each endpoint, with its parameters, response fields and a live example. It's generated from an OpenAPI 3.1 document, which you can fetch at `/openapi.json` to build your own client.

## Feeds for the blog

The [blog](/blog) has RSS, Atom and JSON feeds, like the changelog. Both pages have an RSS button by the title, with Atom and JSON Feed in its menu, and feed readers find the feeds on their own.

## Search

- The command menu now also finds moves, templates, docs pages and their headings, blog posts and changelog entries.
- The menu beside the search field keeps a search within one section, such as Moves or Templates. Before you type, it lists everything in that section.
- The arrow keys stop at the top and bottom of the results instead of wrapping around.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account from the account menu. It removes your bookmarks, submissions, feedback and job posts. If a plan or sponsorship still renews, cancel it in Billing first.
