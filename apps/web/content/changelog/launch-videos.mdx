## Launch Videos

[Launch Videos](/launch-videos) breaks launch films into scenes: each with its role in the story, its creative device, its exact frames and the moves it uses. Watch a film by chapter, loop a single scene, or browse scenes on their own.

## Copy a film or a scene

Pro copies a prompt for any scene or a whole film, written for Remotion, HyperFrames, Editframe or fframes.

- **Adapt** keeps the choreography and swaps in your brand.
- **Exact** rebuilds the film 1:1.

## Storyboards

The [storyboard](/launch-videos/compose) mixes scenes from different films into one video with one brand brief. Change the aspect, frame rate or length and the timing stays frame-exact.

## MCP

Three new Pro tools: `search-launch-scenes`, `get-launch-prompt` and `compose-launch-prompt`.

## Plans

Launch Videos prompts and MCP tools come with every Pro and Business plan, like Moves and templates. Browsing films and scenes is free.

## Templates

Launch Videos replaces the launch film template; its links now open Launch Videos.
