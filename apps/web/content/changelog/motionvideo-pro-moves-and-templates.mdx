## MotionVideo Pro

[Pro and Business](/pro) unlock every copy-ready prompt on the site, the Pro tools in the MotionVideo MCP and the agent skill repository. Business adds seats for your team. Your plan, renewal date and seats live in Billing on your dashboard. If you bought the skill bundle, you keep your access to the repository.

## Moves

[Moves](/moves) is a library of 120 motion design moves, from squash and stretch to trim path loops. Each one explains what the move is, when it works and the common mistakes, with gallery examples where they exist. Pro adds a copy-ready prompt for every move.

## Prompt templates and the customizer

- [Templates](/templates) are tested prompt structures written by MotionVideo, with every part you would change turned into a field. Their full prompts are in Pro.
- [Customize](/customize) any gallery prompt for free: set the subject, size, length, colours and typeface, tune creativity, pace and polish, and copy your version.

## Docs, MCP and the agent skill

- [Docs](/docs) cover getting started, installing the skill, Moves, the MCP, plans and the public API.
- The [MCP](/motionvideo-mcp) and [agent skill](/motionvideo-skill) have their own pages. Signing in to the MCP unlocks its Pro tools for your plan.

## Blog and changelog

The [blog](/blog) shares notes on making motion graphics with AI coding agents, and this [changelog](/changelog) lists what's new.
