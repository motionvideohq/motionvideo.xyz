## GTA 6 Clip Pack

The [GTA 6 Clip Pack](/gta-6-clipping) adds the motion-graphics layer on top of the gameplay clips you already cut in CapCut, Premiere, Resolve or a clip tool: a wanted-level star meter, mission passed, failed and busted style cards, beat-synced kinetic captions, HUD callouts and impact effects, timed to the moments in your clip. It comes in four styles (Vice, Wasted, Neon and Clean) and turns a 16:9 stream with a facecam into the vertical clipper layout.

Your agent finds the moments with ffmpeg and the timestamps you mark, writes an overlay spec and hands it to the renderer skill you already use, from open-source Remotion or HyperFrames skills to the ones in MotionVideo Pro. The pack ships no renderer code, so it works with any of them.

## Price and access

The pack is $49 once and lives in a private GitHub repository. Pro annual, Pro lifetime and Business annual include it. Once you have it, Billing in your dashboard shows the install command and the customer portal where you connect GitHub for the invite. The [docs](/docs/gta-6) cover setup and the overlay spec.
