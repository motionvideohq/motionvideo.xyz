## Logos

Every logo comes as SVG and PNG. The lockup, logomark, and wordmark have versions for light and dark backgrounds; use the sun and moon controls to preview and copy the right one. The kit also includes the yellow logomark and app icon.

<BrandLogos />

## Brand colors

Use black or white for the logo, whichever the background needs, and yellow for the app icon and highlights. The light surface is `#FFFFFF` with black ink (`#000000`); the dark surface is `#0A0A0A` with white ink (`#FFFFFF`). The yellow gradient runs from light yellow (`#FFDD73`) at the top to yellow (`#FFBE25`) at the bottom. Click a swatch to copy its color or CSS gradient.

## Using the name and logo

A few rules keep MotionVideo recognizable.

### One word, capital M and V

Write **MotionVideo**. Not Motion Video, Motionvideo or MOTIONVIDEO.

### Give it room

Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the logomark’s height, and set the logomark 16 px tall or larger.

### Use the files as they are

Pick the version made for your background instead of editing one.

### No endorsements

Don’t use the logo in a way that suggests we endorse your product.
