Launch Videos are launch films broken into scenes. Each scene names its role in the story (hook, reveal, demo, proof, CTA and more), its creative device, its exact frames and the [moves](/moves) it uses. Pro and Business add a copy-ready prompt for every scene and every film.

## Two modes

- **Adapt** keeps every shot, timing, easing and transition, and replaces the original's name, colors, font, logo, copy and UI with your brand. Nothing of the original appears.
- **Exact** rebuilds the film 1:1: the same shots, timing, copy, colors and assets, as editable layers. Make sure you may use the original's assets before you publish the result.

## Renderers

Every prompt is written for the renderer you pick: Remotion, HyperFrames, Editframe or fframes. The timing is the same in each; only the build instructions change.

## Build a storyboard

Add scenes from any film to the [storyboard](/launch-videos/compose), reorder them and copy one prompt for the whole video. One brand brief covers every scene. You can change the aspect, the frame rate or the total length; every scene stretches or tightens by the same factor within its limits, and no frame is lost or doubled at a boundary.

When two scenes can't meet as designed (one opens on a panel the other doesn't end on), the storyboard offers a hard cut or a short bridge shot. A length the scenes can't reach shows the range they can.

## In the MCP

Three Pro tools bring the same prompts to your agent:

- `search-launch-scenes` finds films or scenes by role, style, aspect and length.
- `get-launch-prompt` returns the prompt for a film or one of its scenes.
- `compose-launch-prompt` returns one prompt for an ordered list of scenes.

The same inputs give the same prompt on the site and in the MCP. See [MCP](/docs/mcp) for their parameters.
